Chelmsford Grow-a-Row is a community-driven initiative: neighbors, churches, farms, and local businesses grow an extra row of vegetables in their gardens and donate the harvest to the food-insecure of our community.

As part of the Chelmsford Age-Friendly initiative, the program fosters healthy, nutritious food choices — especially for our elderly population. All Grow-a-Row produce is grown using organic methods, with no chemical sprays for pests or disease.

Who We Serve
Chelmsford Food Pantry
Serves about 140 families every week — roughly half from Chelmsford. Grow-a-Row delivers fresh produce Wednesday, Thursday, and Friday through the growing season.
Table of Plenty
Volunteers serve a free Tuesday-evening meal at the Senior Center to about 125 regular participants, most of them Chelmsford seniors, who select fresh produce before the meal.
Chelmsford Housing Authority
Weekly produce selections for elderly residents of Chelmsford Arms on Smith Street, bringing fresh vegetables directly to seniors aging in place.
The Baseline Season

2025 was the first season in which every Grow-a-Row donation was weighed and recorded — 464 donations from 35 donors across 43 varieties of produce. These figures established the baseline for measuring the program's growth, and the 2026 season went on to exceed them on donations, weight, and value.

A Community-Wide Effort

Thirty-five donors contributed in 2025 — the program's broadest donor base to date: backyard gardeners, the Grow-a-Row community garden (567 pounds), Brooks Automation's employee garden (353 pounds across 76 donations), Cindy Gist, Ralph Mason, Mission Row Hub, and the Wotton Street gardens, among many others.

Every 2025 donation was weighed and recorded at collection, and those records are now preserved in the program’s tracking application — the verifiable baseline for all future seasons.

HOW WE MEASURE

Produce is weighed at collection and valued at the market price per pound in effect on that date. Servings assume 2.5 servings per pound of fresh produce; meal equivalence assumes $3.00 per meal.

We thank the Select Board for its continued support of the Age-Friendly initiative this program proudly serves — and the volunteers and donors who make it all possible.
